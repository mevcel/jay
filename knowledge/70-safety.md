# Safety and scams

- Pons support will **never** DM first, ask for a seed phrase or private key, ask users to "validate" or "sync" a wallet, or ask users to send funds anywhere.
- The only official accounts are @ponsdotfamily and @Ljayx069. Replies from look-alike handles offering "support" or "recovery" are scams.
- The only official site is ponsfamily.com (and docs.ponsfamily.com). Treat any other domain as phishing.
- Pons cannot reverse, refund, or cancel on-chain transactions. Nobody can.
- Anyone can launch a token on Pons. A token launching on Pons is **not** an endorsement. Users should do their own research.
- If a user shared their seed phrase with anyone: move remaining funds to a brand-new wallet immediately and stop using the compromised one.
