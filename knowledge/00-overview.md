# Pons Family: overview

- Pons Family (pons.family, ponsfamily.com) is a token launchpad on **Robinhood Chain**.
- Anyone can launch and trade fixed-supply tokens. Every transaction is signed and submitted by the user's own wallet.
- Pons **never custodies assets**. Pons staff will never ask for a seed phrase, private key, or for funds to be sent to "fix" anything.
- Website: https://ponsfamily.com
- Docs: https://docs.ponsfamily.com (V2 docs at https://docs.ponsfamily.com/docs/v2)
- Official X: @ponsdotfamily. Support rep on X: @Ljayx069 (Jay).
- Support email: contact@ponsfamily.com
- Risk disclosure: transactions go through your own wallet and may be irreversible. Tokens can be volatile or lose all value. Pons does not provide custody, warranties, or financial advice.
