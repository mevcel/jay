# Creator rewards and claiming

- V1: creator rewards accrue inside the token's locked liquidity position. When rewards are available, the creator wallet can claim them at any time from the token page on ponsfamily.com.
- Only the **creator wallet** that launched the token can claim. Switching wallets will show zero claimable.
- V2: fees are not pushed to the wallet as they are earned. They build up as a balance in the fee escrow that the creator withdraws whenever they want, in ETH or the pair token.
- If claimable shows zero: confirm the connected wallet is the creator wallet, confirm the network is Robinhood Chain (chain id 4663), and note that rewards only accrue when there is trading volume.
- Pons can never move or claim fees on a creator's behalf.
